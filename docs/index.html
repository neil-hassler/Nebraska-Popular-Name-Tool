Nebraska Revised Statutes

Popular Name Tool

Find Nebraska laws by their commonly known names. 944 acts indexed and linked to official sources.

Many Nebraska statutes have a “popular name”—a short, everyday title like the Meadowlark Act or the Data Privacy Act. This tool collects all 944 of those names into a single searchable index so you can find the law you’re looking for without knowing its chapter and section number. Each entry links directly to the full text on the Nebraska Legislature’s official website.
